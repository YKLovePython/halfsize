halfsize / WhatsApp

Send a video on WhatsApp without the 16 MB problem

WhatsApp allows 16 MB for videos sent as media. Send a bigger file and it either refuses it or crushes it. halfsize gets the file under the limit first, then measures what the compression actually cost.

Get halfsize Star on GitHub

Step by step

  1. Install Node.js 18+.
  2. Open a terminal where the video is.
  3. Run npx halfsize clip.mp4 --for whatsapp. The --for flag sets 16 MB for you.
  4. Send the generated clip.16mb.mp4 from WhatsApp as a video.
$ npx halfsize clip.mp4 --for whatsapp

Why your WhatsApp videos look worse than the original

WhatsApp compresses every video you send as media. That is fine for a five-second joke and painful for anything with detail: text, faces in low light, landscape footage. Starting from a file that is already at 16 MB gives its encoder less work to do, so the version your family receives is closer to what you shot.

If the recipient needs the exact original, send the video as a document instead of media. It keeps the file intact, but it loses inline playback. For everything else, fitting under 16 MB is the practical option.

Measured on a 60 s 1920×1080 30 fps source at 18 Mbps. Shorter clips do better at the same limit because the bitrate budget is spread over fewer seconds.

Long video, small cap. A 10-minute clip at 16 MB is roughly 200 kbps of video. No encoder can make that look sharp. Cut it into shorter messages, or send it as a document. The VMAF report will tell you when you have crossed that line.

Practical tips

  • Trim before compressing. Every removed second is bits returned to the rest of the clip.
  • Use H.264. It is the default, and it is the format WhatsApp handles most predictably.
  • Do not chase exact bytes. The tool lands 1–3% under the cap on purpose; a file at 15.9 MB is safer than one at 16.01 MB.
  • Batch similar clips. npx halfsize a.mp4 b.mp4 --for whatsapp.

FAQ

What is WhatsApp's video size limit?

16 MB for videos sent as media. Documents can be much larger, but they do not play inline.

Does halfsize upload my video?

No. FFmpeg runs locally. Your file stays on your device; there is no account and no server.

Can I target a different size?

Yes: --to 10mb, --to 16mb, or any value you type. --for whatsapp is only a shortcut.

Will WhatsApp compress it again?

If you send it as a video, yes, lightly. That is unavoidable. Starting under the cap means its second pass has far less work to do.

Does it work on a phone?

The CLI needs a computer. The optional local web app also runs on a computer and is reachable from a phone on the same network if you choose to expose it.
