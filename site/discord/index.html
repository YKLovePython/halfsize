halfsize / Discord

Get a video under Discord's 20 MB limit

Discord's free plan stops at 20 MB per file. Paid tiers raise the cap, but most people just need the clip to fit. This takes one command and runs entirely on your computer.

Get halfsize Star on GitHub

Step by step

  1. Install Node.js 18+ if you do not have it.
  2. Open a terminal in the folder with your video.
  3. Run the command below. --for discord sets the cap to 20 MB for you.
$ npx halfsize clip.mp4 --for discord

  halfsize 0.1.9
  clip.mp4
  136 MB  60s  1920x1080  30.00 fps  h264

  target     20.0 MB   (discord)
  plan       H.264  1280x720  2.46 Mbps  audio 128 kbps  2-pass
  output     clip.20mb.mp4

  measuring quality                                        done in 8s

  136 MB  →  19.45 MB   7.0x smaller   2.7% under target
  VMAF 88.3   SSIM 0.971   slight softening

Then drag clip.20mb.mp4 into the Discord message box and send. That is it.

What 20 MB actually costs in quality

Discord's cap is the same number for everyone, but the quality cost depends on duration and content. Measured on a 60-second 1080p clip:

Source: 60 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 18 Mbps. VMAF at the midpoint against the source. A talking head does better; confetti does worse.

Why not just use Discord's own compression?

Discord re-encodes videos for inline playback, but it will not take a 200 MB file under a 20 MB upload cap. You have to get the file to the limit first. Doing that with a video editor means guessing a bitrate and exporting repeatedly.

halfsize solves the part that is actually annoying: it derives the bitrate from the target and the duration, picks a resolution that fits, and then tells you the measured quality. If the verdict says visible loss, you know to trim the clip or drop to 720p before uploading instead of finding out from your friends.

Keep H.264 for Discord. The default already is H.264, which plays inline everywhere. H.265 makes a smaller file at the same quality, but browser and mobile support is inconsistent, so it is not worth the risk for a chat upload.

Tips that help more than a lower bitrate

  • Trim first. Removing five boring seconds buys more quality than any encoder setting.
  • 720p is usually the sweet spot at 20 MB. The tool picks it automatically for a one-minute clip.
  • Send as a file if the details matter. Inline playback is convenient; a file attachment avoids the extra playback transcode.
  • Batch it. npx halfsize clip1.mp4 clip2.mp4 --for discord handles several files in one run.

FAQ

What is Discord's free upload limit?

20 MB per file. Nitro tiers raise it, but the free limit is what halfsize targets with --for discord.

Does this upload my video to a server?

No. It runs FFmpeg locally. The only network access is the one-time FFmpeg download during installation.

Why is the output 19.45 MB instead of exactly 20 MB?

It aims a little under the cap so container overhead and bitrate variance cannot push you over. You can override the target with --to 20mb.

Can I make it smaller than 20 MB?

Yes: --to 10mb or --to 8mb. The report will tell you whether the result still looks acceptable.

It says "visible loss". What now?

Trim the clip, accept 720p, or switch to --codec h265 if your audience can play it. The tool is telling you the truth instead of hiding it.
