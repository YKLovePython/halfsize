Video size limits, by platform

Every platform publishes a cap, and almost every cap is measured differently from the number your file manager shows. Here are the limits people actually hit, checked in November 2026, and the one command that lands under each of them.

Why the number you see is not the number you get

Three things eat into a cap, and they stack:

  • Decimal versus binary. Platforms mean 20,000,000 bytes. Windows and macOS often show 19.07 MiB and call it 19 MB. A file that “is 20 MB” can already be over.
  • Base64 for email. Binary attachments are encoded as text, 3 bytes into 4. A 20 MB file becomes a 26.7 MB message and bounces at a 25 MB limit. Compress to 18 MB for Gmail, 15 MB for a 20 MB mailbox.
  • Container and audio. The cap applies to the whole file, not the video stream. Muxing overhead and the audio track are part of the budget, which is why the planner subtracts them first.

Rule of thumb: aim 5% under the advertised cap for a normal upload, and divide by 1.37 first for an email attachment. --for already does both.

One command per cap

$ npx halfsize holiday.mp4 --for discord

  target     20.0 MB
  plan       H.264  1280x720  2.46 Mbps  audio 128 kbps  2-pass
  scale      1920x1080 → 1280x720
  output     holiday.discord.mp4

  136 MB  →  19.45 MB   7x smaller   2.8% under target
  VMAF 88.3   SSIM 0.971   slight softening

Swap the preset for the platform you are fighting: --for whatsapp, --for wechat, --for gmail, --for outlook, --for twitter. Anything else becomes --to 800mb or --to 700k.

VMAF above 90 reads as “looks the same”, 80–90 as slight softening, 70–80 as visible loss. Real numbers move with content: a talking head does better than confetti.

When the target is arithmetically impossible

A cap is a bitrate budget: bytes × 8 ÷ seconds. One hour inside 20 MB is 44 kbps for video and audio combined, which no encoder can make look good. Since 0.1.9 the tool says so before encoding and reports the smallest size that duration can hold, instead of spending ten minutes to hand back a file that still does not fit. Trimming the clip is the honest fix.

FAQ

Why does a 20 MB file fail on a 20 MB limit?

Decimal megabytes versus binary mebibytes, base64 for email, and container overhead all count. Aim 5% under, or use the preset which already does.

What is Discord's free upload limit?

20 MB per file on the free plan as of November 2026. Nitro raises it; --to overrides the preset.

Does WhatsApp re-compress videos?

Yes, when you send video as media. Sending it as a document skips the 16 MB media cap and the second encode, but the recipient gets a file rather than a preview.

Can it hit a 2 GB target?

Yes, if the source has the bitrate to spend. A short clip cannot grow to fill a big cap, and a very long one cannot shrink into a tiny cap.

Is anything uploaded?

No. FFmpeg runs on your machine. No account, no quota, no watermark.
